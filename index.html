Why SAPATOO

Sapato is Portuguese for shoe. Portuguese traders brought the word to Malacca in the 1500s, and it never left: in Indonesian, a shoe is still a sepatu.

Plenty of everyday words made the same trip. A party is a pesta, from festa. Sunday is Minggu, from domingo. A window is a jendela, from janela.

We liked that a word for shoe could walk that far. So the shop took the old spelling, with an extra O for the pair, and every shoe on our shelves is named after another word that made the journey.

We sell sneakers, running shoes, boots and dress shoes for men, women and children, from EU 19 to 47. If a size is wrong, bring it back or send it back and we will change it.

Before you buy

How do I order? Pick pairs on the price list, choose their sizes and add them to your bag. At checkout the order goes to us on WhatsApp, and we reply with the total and a payment link. No WhatsApp? The checkout can send it by email to hello@sapatoo.example.

How do I find my size? Measure your foot from heel to longest toe and put the length on the slip above. Between two sizes, take the larger one.

Can I change the size? Yes. Bring or send the pair back unworn within 30 days and we send the right size free.

How long does delivery take? Orders placed before 14.00 leave the same day. They reach most of Java in two to four days and the other islands in four to seven.

How much is delivery? Free on orders over Rp 500.000. Below that, the courier’s rate is shown before you pay.

How can I pay? Bank transfer, card or e-wallet online, and cash or card in the shop.

Can I collect in the shop? Yes. Choose collection when you order and we keep the pair for you for seven days.

Do you make wide fits? Minggu, Pesiar and Sekolah also come in a wide fit. Ask for it when you order.

How often should children’s feet be measured? Every three months until they are about ten. Children’s sizes run from EU 19 to 38.

How do I look after leather? Wipe it with a damp cloth, let it dry away from the sun, and polish it once a month.

Can I return a pair for a refund? Yes, unworn and within 30 days. The money goes back the way you paid.
